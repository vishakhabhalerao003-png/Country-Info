!MY FAVORITE COUNTRIES!



01.TURKEY


Capital: Ankara

Ankara is the capital city of Turkey. Located in the central part of Anatolia, the city is an important centre of government and modern Turkish life. Ankara is Turkey's second-largest city by population after Istanbul. The Kocatepe Mosque (Turkish: Kocatepe Camii) is one of the most famous landmarks in Ankara. It was built between 1967 and 1987 in the Kocatepe quarter in Kızılay.

Famous city: Istanbul

Istanbul is the largest city in Turkey and an important economic, cultural and historical centre. It is a city on two continents, with part of the city in Europe and part in Asia. Istanbul lies beside the Bosphorus, between the Sea of Marmara and the Black Sea. The city has a very long history and was known as Byzantium and later Constantinople. It was the capital of several empires before the capital of modern Turkey was moved to Ankara.

Location: Between Europe and Asia, surrounded by the Black Sea, Aegean Sea and Mediterranean Sea.

Turkey covers an area of 783,562 square kilometres. Its Asian side covers most of its surface and is often called Anatolia. The European part of Turkey is known as Eastern Thrace. Turkey is surrounded by seas on three sides and is bordered by several countries including Greece, Bulgaria, Georgia, Armenia, Iran, Iraq and Syria.

Language: Turkish

Currency: Turkish Lira (₺)

Famous for: Turkey also known as "Türkiye" is famous for its rich history, beautiful mosques and palaces, traditional culture, delicious food, colourful bazaars and unique landscapes. It is also known for its ancient ruins, historical architecture, Turkish tea and coffee, beautiful coastlines and traditional arts. The country is famous for landmarks such as Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Grand Bazaar and the ancient city of Ephesus. Ankara, the capital city, is an important centre of government and modern Turkish life, while Istanbul is famous for its unique location between Europe and Asia. Turkish cuisine is also popular around the world and includes dishes such as kebabs, baklava, meze and traditional Turkish breakfast.




02.ITALY


Capital: Rome

Rome is the capital city and most populated municipality of Italy. It is famous for its ancient history and monuments. The Colosseum (Italian: Colosseo) is one of the most famous landmarks in Rome. Another important landmark is Castel Sant'Angelo, which was originally built as a mausoleum for Emperor Hadrian.

Famous city: Venice

Venice is a famous coastal city in northeastern Italy and the capital of the Veneto region. It is built on many islands separated by canals and connected by numerous bridges. Venice is especially famous for its canals, bridges and gondolas. The city is also known for its beautiful historic buildings.

Location: Southern Europe, extending into the Mediterranean Sea.

Italy is located in Southern Europe. It includes the Italian Peninsula and the large islands of Sicily and Sardinia. Italy shares borders with France, Switzerland, Austria and Slovenia. The independent countries of Vatican City and San Marino are located within Italy.

Language: Italian

Currency: Euro (€)

Famous for: Italy also called "Italia" is famous for its ancient history, art, architecture, fashion, pizza, pasta and beautiful cities. It is also known for its museums, Renaissance art, historic monuments, beautiful countryside and rich culinary traditions. Italy is home to famous landmarks such as the Colosseum, the Leaning Tower of Pisa, the Trevi Fountain and the canals of Venice. Rome, the capital city, is famous for its ancient monuments and historical importance, while Venice is known for its canals, bridges and beautiful architecture. Italian cuisine is loved around the world and is especially famous for pizza, pasta, gelato and many regional dishes.




03.INDIA


Capital: New Delhi

New Delhi is the capital city of India and an important centre of government and administration. The India Gate is a famous war memorial in New Delhi. It was designed by Edwin Lutyens and is one of the best-known landmarks of the city. New Delhi is also home to important government buildings including the Parliament House and the Rashtrapati Bhavan.

Famous city: Mumbai

Mumbai is the capital city of the Indian state of Maharashtra and an important financial and commercial centre of India. It is located on the west coast of India along the Arabian Sea. Mumbai is famous for its film industry, busy streets, historic buildings and beautiful coastline. The Gateway of India is one of Mumbai's most famous landmarks.

Location: South Asia.

India is located in South Asia. It is surrounded by the Arabian Sea to the west, the Bay of Bengal to the east and the Indian Ocean to the south. India shares land borders with several countries including Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh and Myanmar. The country has a great variety of landscapes including the Himalayas, plains, deserts, plateaus and coastal regions.

Languages: India has many languages; Hindi and English are widely used for communication at the national level.

Currency: Indian Rupee (₹)

Famous for: India also called "Bharat" is known for its enormous cultural diversity, history, festivals, food, traditional arts, monuments, languages and beautiful landscapes. It is also famous for its many traditions, historic forts, temples, palaces, classical and folk arts and diverse regional cuisines. India has many famous landmarks including the Taj Mahal, the Red Fort, India Gate and the Gateway of India. New Delhi, the capital city, is an important centre of government, history and culture, while Mumbai is famous for its film industry, financial importance and historic buildings. Indian cuisine is famous for its variety of spices, flavours and regional dishes such as biryani, dosa, samosa and many different types of sweets.




04.SPAIN


Capital: Madrid

Madrid is the capital and largest city of Spain. It is an important centre of government, business, culture and art. The city is known for its beautiful squares, museums and historic buildings. The Royal Palace of Madrid is one of the city's most famous landmarks.

Famous city: Barcelona

Barcelona is a famous city in northeastern Spain and the capital of Catalonia. It is known for its architecture, art, beaches and lively culture. The city is especially famous for the works of architect Antoni Gaudí. The Sagrada Família is one of Barcelona's most famous landmarks.

Location: Southwestern Europe.

Spain is located in southwestern Europe on the Iberian Peninsula. It is bordered by Portugal and France and is surrounded by the Atlantic Ocean and Mediterranean Sea. Spain also includes the Balearic Islands and Canary Islands.

Language: Spanish

Currency: Euro (€)

Famous for: Spain also known as "España" is famous for its rich history, beautiful architecture, music, dance, festivals, football and delicious food. It is especially known for flamenco, traditional Spanish festivals, beautiful beaches and historic cities. Spain has many famous landmarks such as the Sagrada Família, the Alhambra and the Royal Palace of Madrid. Madrid, the capital city, is an important centre of Spanish culture and government, while Barcelona is famous for its unique architecture, art and Mediterranean coastline. Spanish cuisine is also popular around the world and is known for dishes such as paella, tapas and tortilla española.




05.GREECE


Capital: Athens

Athens is the capital and largest city of Greece. It is one of the world's oldest cities and has a very important role in ancient history. The Acropolis of Athens is one of the most famous ancient landmarks in the world. The Parthenon is located on the Acropolis and is an important symbol of ancient Greek civilisation.

Famous city: Santorini

Santorini is one of the most famous Greek islands in the Aegean Sea. It is known for its white buildings, blue domes, beautiful views and sunsets. The island is a popular destination because of its unique landscape and traditional villages.

Location: Southeastern Europe.

Greece is located in southeastern Europe. It has many islands and is surrounded by the Aegean Sea, Ionian Sea and Mediterranean Sea. Greece shares borders with Albania, North Macedonia, Bulgaria and Turkey. The country has many mountains, islands and coastal areas.

Language: Greek

Currency: Euro (€)

Famous for: Greece also known as "Elláda" is famous for its ancient history, Greek mythology, beautiful islands, architecture, beaches and traditional food. It is known as an important part of the history of Western civilisation and is famous for ancient monuments and archaeological sites. The country has many famous landmarks including the Acropolis of Athens, the Parthenon and the ancient ruins of Delphi. Athens, the capital city, is famous for its ancient monuments and historical importance, while Santorini is known for its white buildings, blue domes and beautiful sunsets. Greek cuisine is also famous for foods such as Greek salad, moussaka, souvlaki and other traditional Mediterranean dishes.
